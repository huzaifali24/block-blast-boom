Block Bloom Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short: Block Bloom has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details. Your game progress is saved only on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect device information (such as your advertising ID) to show and measure those ads, based on the choices you make.

1. Who we are

Block Bloom (the "App") is a puzzle game published by Kodex Solution ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the App and what choices you have. It applies to the App only, not to other websites or services.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive personal information on our own servers. The App does not have sign-in, accounts, chat or any feature where you type in personal details.

Game data stored on your device. To save your game, the App stores the following locally on your phone: best scores and totals, coins, the daily challenge calendar and streaks, achievements and missions progress, purchased board and piece themes, and settings (sound, haptics, theme). This data never leaves your device through us. It is removed when you uninstall the App or use "Reset progress" in Settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads may appear between games, and you can choose to watch rewarded ads for in-game rewards such as an extra piece or a board-clearing booster.

To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect information from your device, including:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system, language and app version
  • Ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own policies. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your consent and choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). If you are in a region where consent is required, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Me › Privacy Preferences.
  • US state privacy rights. Where applicable, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising through the same Privacy Preferences option.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).
  • Game data. You can delete all game data at any time with Reset progress in Settings or by uninstalling the App.

5. In-app purchases

If the App offers purchases (for example, coin packs or removing ads), payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card or billing details. Google Play shares only what is needed to confirm the purchase. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles payment information.

7. Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies. Data sent between the App and Google is encrypted in transit.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal information ourselves, most of these rights are exercised through the in-app Privacy Preferences, your device settings, or directly with Google. You may also contact us with any request, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the App gains new features. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be announced in the App.
