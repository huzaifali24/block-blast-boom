Block Boom Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026 · Last updated: 27 November 2026

In short: Block Boom does not ask for your name, email or other personal details, and we do not run any servers that collect them. Your progress is saved on your phone and, only if you sign in to Google Play Games, also in your own Google account so you can restore it. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect device information (such as your advertising ID) to show and measure those ads, based on the choices you make.

1. Who we are

Block Boom (the "App") is a puzzle game published by Kodex Solution ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the App and what choices you have. It applies to the App only, not to other websites or services.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive personal information on our own servers. The App has no chat and no feature where you type in personal details.

Game data stored on your device. To save your game, the App stores the following on your phone: scores and totals, coins and power-ups, Adventure levels and stars, the daily challenge, login rewards and streaks, achievements and missions progress, purchased board themes and block skins, and settings (music, sound, vibration, reminders, language). It is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data in your phone's settings.

3. Google Play Games (optional)

You can choose to sign in to Google Play Games. If you do, the App uses it to:

  • Leaderboards and achievements: your scores, Adventure stars and unlocked achievements are sent to Google Play Games and shown with your Play Games profile name.
  • Cloud save: a copy of the game data listed above is saved in your Google account ("Saved Games") so your progress comes back on a new phone or after reinstalling.

This data is stored by Google, linked to your Google account, under the Google Privacy Policy. We cannot see your account details. You can play without signing in, and you can delete your Play Games data from the Google Play Games app or your Google account.

4. Notifications

If you allow it, the App shows a daily reminder when a new daily challenge is ready. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no data is sent anywhere to deliver them. You can turn them off in the App under Profile › Settings › Daily reminders or in your phone's settings.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads may appear between games, and you can choose to watch rewarded ads for in-game rewards such as a power-up, a revive or an extra spin of the Lucky Wheel.

To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect information from your device, including:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system, language and app version
  • Ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own policies. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Your consent and choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). Where consent is required, the App asks for it through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Profile › Privacy preferences.
  • US state privacy rights. Where applicable, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising through the same Privacy preferences option.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).
  • Game data. You can delete all game data on your phone by clearing the App's data in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the App. If you used cloud save, you can also delete the saved game from Google Play Games.

7. In-app purchases

The App offers optional purchases (coin packs and removing ads). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card or billing details; Google Play shares only what is needed to confirm the purchase. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles payment information.

9. Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Cloud saves, leaderboard and achievement data stay in your Google account until you delete them. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies. Data sent between the App and Google is encrypted in transit.

10. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal information ourselves, most of these rights are exercised through the in-app Privacy preferences, your device settings, or directly with Google. You may also contact us with any request, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the App gains new features. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be announced in the App.
