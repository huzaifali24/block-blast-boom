## Full description (max 4000)
Block Boom is a bright, relaxing block puzzle with a satisfying BOOM. Drag pieces onto the 8×8 board, fill rows and columns to blast them away, and chain clears together for huge combo scores. Easy to learn, hard to put down!

🧩 SIMPLE TO PLAY
• Drag a piece onto the board. The spot it will land on glows, and lines you are about to clear light up before you drop.
• Fill a whole row or column to blast it.
• No timer and no rush. The game ends only when none of your pieces fit.

💥 BIG COMBOS, BIG SCORES
• Clear several lines at once, or clear on back-to-back moves, to build a combo.
• Watch the board explode with light beams, sparkles and shockwaves.
• Clear the whole board for an All Clear bonus.
• Great moves even earn coins as you play.

🗺️ ADVENTURE MODE
• 30 hand-tuned levels on a colorful map.
• Reach a score, clear a number of lines, or collect sparkling gems.
• Earn up to 3 stars per level and unlock the next.

🚀 POWER-UPS AND REVIVES
• Undo a move, Bomb a 3×3 area, Rotate a piece, or Shuffle for new pieces.
• Out of space? Revive once per game and keep your score going.

🎁 DAILY FUN
• Daily Challenge: the same board for every player, every day.
• 7-day login rewards that grow every day.
• A Lucky Spin wheel with a free spin daily.
• Daily missions and 24 achievements to unlock.

🎨 MAKE IT YOURS
• Glossy candy blocks, glowing Neon, carved Wood and more block skins.
• Board themes from deep Indigo to Ocean, Forest and Sunset.

✨ AND MORE
• Chill original music that speeds up when the board gets tight.
• Leaderboards, achievements and cloud save with Google Play Games.
• Play offline, anytime.
• Available in English and Urdu.

Block Boom is free to play, with optional in-app purchases (coins, remove ads) and ads.
